Hi, I’m Philip, also known as shawn-phy. I’m a computer engineer working in machine learning and cybersecurity.

I can’t think of an electronic device I’ve owned that I haven’t opened up to **peek inside**. By the time I was nine, an engineering degree already seemed inevitable.

I like working across hardware, security and systems development because I like starting from first principles: understanding what’s happening **underneath the abstractions**, then building from there. That habit has saved me plenty of debugging hours.

I’m always looking for stubborn problems, whether in competitions or hackathons. Some attempts have admittedly been flops; others have become projects I’m proud of.

Off the keyboard, I enjoy playing *chess*, *cycling* and *fixing electronics*.
