- What pulled you into engineering, and what keeps you there?
- Why do you like working across hardware, security and machine learning at once?
- What kind of problem do you want your next role to hand you?
- What do you do away from the keyboard? (Chess, CTFs, cycling, music production…)

Keep it under about 200 words. Markdown works here: **bold**, *italics* and [links](https://github.com/shawn-phy).
